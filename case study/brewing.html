Golden Extraction Parameters

Brew Ratio

1 : 15

Water Temp

90°C - 96°C

3-Step Pour-Over Guide

Pour 30-50ml of water and let sit for 30s to release CO₂.

Slowly pour the remaining water in concentric circles. Keep the coffee bed flat and even.

Wait for the coffee to drip completely. Total extraction time is about 2:00 to 2:30.
